# Credit Risk Addendum (synthetic)

This addendum covers credit limit assignment and monitoring for commercial customers of Atlas Payments.

An automated decision may propose a risk rating using KYC attributes, payment history and bureau data. A human underwriter must approve any adverse credit decision before it takes effect.

Personal data used for credit scoring is limited to identity, beneficial owners and payment performance. Marketing profiles are out of scope.

Default events include insolvency filing and 30-day past-due status on two consecutive invoices.

Governing law: Mexico. Regulator mapping: CNBV information-security expectations for the institution's own controls; this sample is not a regulated product filing.
