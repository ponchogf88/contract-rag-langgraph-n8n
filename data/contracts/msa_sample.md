# Master Services Agreement (synthetic)

This Master Services Agreement governs professional and technology services between Provider and Customer.

Statements of Work will describe scope, fees and service levels. In case of conflict, this MSA prevails over a SOW except for fees and delivery dates.

Limitation of liability: each party's aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, except for confidentiality breaches, IP infringement indemnity and willful misconduct. This Agreement does not provide unlimited liability.

Automatic renewal: the MSA auto-renews for successive one-year terms unless a party gives 60 days' written notice.

Governing law: State of Delaware, USA.

Insurance: Provider maintains professional liability coverage of at least USD 2,000,000.
