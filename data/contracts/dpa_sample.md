# Data Processing Addendum (synthetic)

This Data Processing Addendum applies where Provider processes personal data on behalf of Customer.

Roles: Customer is controller. Provider is processor. GDPR and Mexican LFPDPPP principles apply to the extent of their respective scope.

Purpose limitation: personal data is processed only for contract performance, support and mandatory legal obligations. No training of foundation models on Customer personal data by default.

Sub-processors: Provider may appoint sub-processors listed in Annex B after 15 days' prior notice. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

International transfers: transfers outside Mexico or the EEA use Standard Contractual Clauses plus a transfer impact assessment. Transfers are not unrestricted and are not permitted to any country without a valid mechanism.

Security: encryption in transit and at rest, access logging, 72-hour incident notice.

Data subject rights: Provider assists Customer with access, deletion and portability requests within 10 business days.

Governing law of this DPA: European Union GDPR as to EU data subjects; Mexican law as to Mexican data subjects.
